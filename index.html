Research

Emerging Markets Equity  ·  June 2026

NU Holdings Ltd — Investment View

Buy

An independent equity analysis of NU Holdings (NYSE: NU), the largest digital bank in Latin America by customers. The note examines Brazil's macro backdrop, the structural shift in its banking sector driven by Pix and digital entrants, and NU's evolution from card issuer to full-stack financial platform. It argues that the recent drawdown from ~$18 to ~$12 reflects a sentiment reset rather than a fundamental one, and that the current valuation — approximately 21× 2025 earnings for a business compounding at 30%+ ROE — represents an attractive entry point for patient investors.
